Is getting a smartphone earlier associated with higher rates of anxiety, depression, alcohol use, and vaping?

This graph show the percentage of grade 11 and 12 students screening positive for anxiety or depression, or reporting drinking alcohol or vaping in the past year.

Use the slider to see how getting a smartphone earlier or later affects each outcome. You can also select a specific outcome to see gender differences.

What we found

  • Youth who got a smartphone earlier were more likely to screen positive for anxiety and depression, and more likely to use alcohol or vaping products in grades 11 and 12.
  • Youth who got a smartphone later were less likely to screen positive for anxiety and depression, and less likely to use alcohol or vaping products in grades 11 and 12.
